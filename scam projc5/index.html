Help and Information

Learn about phishing, scams, and how to stay safe online

What is Phishing?

Phishing is a cyber attack where criminals impersonate legitimate organizations to steal sensitive information.

  • Fake emails from banks
  • SMS claiming account blocked
  • Fake websites mimicking real ones

What is a Cyber Scam?

A cyber scam is a fraudulent scheme conducted online to deceive people and steal money or personal data.

  • Lottery and Prize scams
  • Fake job offers
  • QR code scams

Stay Protected

Cybersecurity Tips

  1. Never share OTP or passwords
  2. Verify URLs before clicking
  3. Beware of false urgency
  4. Use strong unique passwords
